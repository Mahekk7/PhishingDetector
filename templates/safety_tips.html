Everyday cyber-safety tips

Use these checks before clicking links, scanning QR codes, sending money, or sharing personal information.

1. Check the full web address

Look for misspellings, extra words, unusual subdomains, and domains imitating trusted organizations. HTTPS alone does not prove legitimacy.

2. Never share OTPs or passwords

Do not disclose your password, PIN, CVV, or one-time verification code to someone contacting you unexpectedly.

3. Verify unexpected requests

Contact the organization using its official app, website, or phone number rather than using details in a suspicious message.

4. Be careful with QR codes

Preview the destination and verify unexpected payment requests before proceeding.

5. Watch for pressure tactics

Pause when a message threatens account closure, demands immediate action, or promises guaranteed returns.

6. Keep devices updated

Update your browser and operating system. Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication where available.

7. Report suspicious activity

Keep relevant evidence and report suspected fraud to the platform, bank, or appropriate official service.

8. Remember tool limitations

Automated tools can produce false positives and false negatives. “Likely Safe” is not a guarantee.

Quick checklist

  • Did I expect this message?
  • Does the sender address match the official domain?
  • Is it asking for money, OTPs, passwords, or personal data?
  • Can I verify through an official channel?
